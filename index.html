Customers are obligated to pay for their committed volumes whether they take delivery or not" "These are take or pay agreements and there are no contractual outs for our customers from these agreements" Regarding historical contracts breaking: "These LTAs that we used to have never had any binding terms." The new SCAs contain extensive contractual obligations Regarding Elon's claims on Softbank defaulting on Turbines: Question from Morgan Stanley to $GE Vernova CEO on whether they see cancellations or financing difficulties with counterparties on their turbine orders: “No… we are not” TLDR: - Micron states LTAs/SCAs are noncancellable take/pay (where counterparties are hyperscalers). And that these types are different from historical memory cancellations. - On Softbank turbine orders, GE Vernova CEO Scott Strazik said it wasn't seeing customer financing disruptions, requests to exit turbine reservations, or slowdowns. Maybe Elon's claims are more narrow, but should be evaluated more closely given competitive interests vs. Softbank/OpenAI.

繁體中文： Serenity 提供關於記憶體與 Softbank 渦輪機點評論的產業評論。針對「價格下跌時 LTA 無意義」的說法：$MU 表示戰略客戶協議不能單方面取消，客戶有義務支付承諾量（無論是否取貨）；這些是 take-or-pay 協議，客戶沒有合約退出條款。歷史合約從未有約束條款，新的 SCA 包含廣泛合約義務。針對 Elon 關於 Softbank 渦輪機違約的說法：Morgan Stanley 詢問 $GE Vernova CEO 是否看到取消或融資困難，答案為「沒有」。TLDR：Micron 表示 LTA/SCA 是不可取消的 take/pay（對方為超大規模業者），與歷史記憶體取消不同；GE Vernova CEO Scott Strazik 表示未看到客戶融資中斷、退出預訂或放緩。建議更詳細評估 Elon 的說法（考慮競爭利益）。

English: Serenity provides industry commentary on recent claims regarding memory and Softbank turbine defaulting. On claims that LTAs are "meaningless while prices fall": $MU states strategic customer agreements cannot be unilaterally canceled; customers are obligated to pay for committed volumes whether they take delivery or not; these are take-or-pay agreements with no contractual outs. Historical LTAs never had binding terms; new SCAs contain extensive contractual obligations. On Elon's claims about Softbank defaulting on turbines: Morgan Stanley asked $GE Vernova CEO if they see cancellations or financing difficulties; answer was "No… we are not." TLDR: Micron states LTAs/SCAs are noncancellable take/pay (counterparties are hyperscalers), different from historical cancellations. GE Vernova CEO Scott Strazik said not seeing customer financing disruptions, requests to exit turbine reservations, or slowdowns. Elon's claims may be narrower but should be evaluated more closely given competitive interests vs. Softbank/OpenAI.
