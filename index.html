原文摘要 / Original Summary: $NVDA earnings transcript + industry read through. To sum it up: "Demand far exceeds supply". Good news for the $MU / Samsung memory longs: supply commitments increased from $279B last quarter to $390B, primarily related to the multi-year procurement of memory supply (from SEC filings). Major leap Q/Q. Revenue expected to grow approximately 75% year-over-year, up from 70% (upward revision, likely due to capacity constraints easing). New agreements with Kinsus and Samsung Electro-Mechanics support additional ABF substrate capacity. Industry cloud backlog increased from $2 trillion to $2.5 trillion in a single quarter. GPU rental pricing rising dramatically (Hopper GPUs: 2x original pricing; B300 GPUs: +60% since introduction). NVIDIA says its own compute is fully utilized across hyperscalers and neoclouds. Co-packaged optics roadmap remains on track. Optical networking growing rapidly ($21B+ in quarterly networking); CPO scale-out switches entered volume production (Lambda announced production deployment of Nvidia photonics CPO switches). Additional recurring economics from Neoclouds on upfront sale of infrastructure. Points to industry acceleration from memory to CPO to neoclouds, relative to markets pricing in collapse.
