Jensen Huang on memory supply through 2029

Status ID: 2113433338522546399 · 2026-10-23 00:52 UTC · Original post by @aleabitoreddit
$NVDA Jensen Huang: Surplus of memory supply is unlikely by 2029. ... "I don't think so" in terms of possibilities of oversupply in 2029. ... Nvidia probably has the most visibility in the entire industry. ... Samsung already allocated majority of capacity into 2031 ... hyperscalers like $GOOGL are already reportedly securing memory LTAs into 2035 ... Yet there's endless "it's all over" sentiment on memory now. I'd probably trust Jensen's opinion here.

繁體中文： Serenity 引述 Jensen Huang 指出 2029 年前記憶體供應過剩不大可能。回應韓國報紙預測 2029 年成為紅海的看法。Jensen 強調記憶體可以一直用得更多（例如資料中心 LPDDR）。Serenity 個人認為 Nvidia 具有行業最佳能見度，並提及 Samsung 已將大部分產能配置至 2031、$GOOGL 等超級雲端已報導取得至 2035 的記憶體長約。相對於目前「記憶體已結束」的情緒，她較信任 Jensen 的看法。

English: Serenity highlights Jensen Huang stating that a surplus of memory supply is unlikely by 2029, responding “I don't think so” to a Korean newspaper view of a 2029 red ocean. Jensen notes you can always use more memory (citing LPDDR in data centers). Serenity’s personal view is that Nvidia has the best industry visibility; she notes Samsung capacity already allocated majority into 2031 and reports of hyperscalers such as $GOOGL securing memory LTAs into 2035. She would trust Jensen’s opinion over the current “it’s all over” sentiment on memory.

Reuters piece on global selloff and hedge-fund deleveraging

Status ID: 2113575601390936238 · 2026-10-23 10:17 UTC · Original post by @aleabitoreddit
Pretty crazy Reuters piece on the global selloff. - October 22 marked the 1st time since the COVID crash that long/short, systematic, and multi-strategy hedge funds tracked by Goldman Sachs ALL lost more than 1% in a single day. - Tech-focused hedge funds: down ~10% in October - Asia-focused hedge funds: down ~9% in October ... retail has been the ones buying the dip in semiconductor, neocloud, and memory stocks. And the stocks bought by most retail were $NBIS, $SNDK, and $TSM. ... institutional deleveraging events among hedge funds. Quite the divergence, I do think there’s been a ton of irrational selling now, hedge fund deleveraging seems to be a major contributor.

繁體中文： Serenity 討論 Reuters 關於全球撤售的報導。10 月 22 日是自 COVID 崩盤以來，Goldman Sachs 追蹤的 long/short、systematic 與 multi-strategy 對沖基金全部在單日虧損超過 1% 的第一次。科技型對沖基金 10 月約跌 10%，亞洲型約跌 9%。Citadel / JP Morgan 等指出零售在買入半導體、neocloud 與記憶體的跌幅，買入最多的是 $NBIS、$SNDK 與 $TSM。Reuters 指出此次跌幅觸發機構去槓桿。Serenity 看到明顯分化，認為目前有大量非理性撤售，對沖基金去槓桿是主要貢獻者。

English: Serenity discusses a Reuters piece on the global selloff. October 22 was the first time since the COVID crash that long/short, systematic, and multi-strategy hedge funds tracked by Goldman Sachs all lost more than 1% in a single day. Tech-focused hedge funds down ~10% in October; Asia-focused down ~9%. Separate interviews (Citadel / JP Morgan) indicate retail has been buying the dip in semiconductor, neocloud, and memory stocks, with $NBIS, $SNDK, and $TSM among the most bought. Reuters stated the downturn triggered institutional deleveraging. Serenity sees a clear divergence and believes there has been substantial irrational selling, with hedge-fund deleveraging as a major contributor.
