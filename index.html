Counterpoint on $MRVL / $LITE acquisition: $SIVE as new CW/ELS capacity; laser sourcing diversification and module makers moving upstream

Status ID: 2117550199895896490 · 2026-11-03 09:31 UTC · Original post by @aleabitoreddit
Counterpoint on $MRVL / $LITE acqusition: - "Every buyer that competes with Marvell will want a second, independent laser source". They identify $SIVE / Scintil Photonics as new CW / ELS capacity, with names like $CRDO as potential partners of Sivers. - "With Lumentum sold out for years, the overflow goes to whoever has merchant capacity" Already said this before, but we'll likely see some incentive to diversify laser sourcing. Sivers was cited here, and comes online with 100M lasers / external foundry capacity (2:1 external/internal target). To demonstrate the sheer scale on that, $AAOI exit 2026 laser capacity target was roughly ~24M/year (but different blend). - "Module makers move upstream" was also another second order effect. $JBL as one example is now heavily competing with the combined Lumentum + Marvell.... There's other players like $SMTC that are explicitly doing M&A for expanding laser capacity. I don't think the overall AI valuation crash is representative of individual company strength in the optical sector. There's very compelling opportunities in the laser bottleneck in the upcoming year that I believe markets are mispricing.

繁體中文： Serenity 提出對 $MRVL / $LITE 收購的反駁觀點。她指出「每個與 Marvell 競爭的買家都會想要第二個獨立的雷射來源」。他們將 $SIVE / Scintil Photonics 識別為新的 CW / ELS 產能，並提到 $CRDO 等作為 Sivers 的潛在合作夥伴。「Lumentum 已售罄多年，溢出需求將流向擁有商用產能的任何人」。她之前已說過，我們可能會看到分散雷射來源的激勵。Sivers 被引用，並將上線 1 億顆雷射 / 外部晶圓代工產能（外部/內部目標 2:1）。為展示規模，$AAOI 2026 年底雷射產能目標約為每年約 2400 萬（但混合不同）。「模組製造商向上游移動」是另一個次級效應。$JBL 作為一例現在正與合併後的 Lumentum + Marvell 激烈競爭……還有其他玩家如 $SMTC 明確透過併購擴展雷射產能。她不認為整體 AI 估值崩跌代表光學領域個別公司的實力。她相信明年雷射瓶頸中有非常引人注目的機會，市場正在錯誤定價。

English: Serenity offers a counterpoint on the $MRVL / $LITE acquisition. She notes that "Every buyer that competes with Marvell will want a second, independent laser source." They identify $SIVE / Scintil Photonics as new CW / ELS capacity, with names like $CRDO as potential partners of Sivers. "With Lumentum sold out for years, the overflow goes to whoever has merchant capacity." She has said this before, but we'll likely see some incentive to diversify laser sourcing. Sivers was cited here, and comes online with 100M lasers / external foundry capacity (2:1 external/internal target). To demonstrate the sheer scale, $AAOI exit 2026 laser capacity target was roughly ~24M/year (but different blend). "Module makers move upstream" was also another second order effect. $JBL as one example is now heavily competing with the combined Lumentum + Marvell. There are other players like $SMTC that are explicitly doing M&A for expanding laser capacity. She doesn't think the overall AI valuation crash is representative of individual company strength in the optical sector. There's very compelling opportunities in the laser bottleneck in the upcoming year that she believes markets are mispricing.
