Various commentary from Qualcomm, Arm, to Meta executives this week during the AI selloff; no evidence of widespread AI demand destruction

Status ID: 2119458038260318634 · 2026-11-08 15:52 UTC · Original post by @aleabitoreddit

Traditional Chinese: Serenity 彙整本週 AI 賣壓期間高通、Arm 至 Meta 等高管評論。$LITE CEO 認同 $IREN CEO：需求帳本與市場表現存在巨大脫節，卻出現大規模賣壓（11/6）。$AMD CEO（CNBC）：正處於採用週期初期，需求高於供應（11/4）。$ARM CEO：若美國在資料中心建設猶豫，世界其他地方準備接手，需求巨大，美國需與中國競爭，中國未放緩（11/5）。$APLD CEO：銀行建設貸款、專案融資與債券市場仍可用，儘管利率與信用利差上升；最大風險是準時交付，需求圖景極為強勁（11/2）。$QCOM CEO：不是需求受限而是供應受限；晶圓、基板、組裝皆短缺因需求極高；市場反應的是算力建設速度（相關）。$META 論政治風險：有州長以資料中心為競選平台，勝選後表示願意會面以免投資與工作流失（11/4）；即使先前反對的政治人物也在談判，但 Meta 總裁強調新美國資料中心將更難啟動。TLDR：加速器、網路與半導體公司評論極度看多；資料中心開發商報告強勁定價/需求，但更強調融資、許可與執行風險（較謹慎）；先前反對資料中心的政治人物顯示合作。需求消失與因無法快速建設而延遲有重要區別。儘管賣壓嚴重，評論中未見廣泛 AI 需求毀滅證據。

English: Serenity compiles various commentary from Qualcomm, Arm, to Meta executives this week during the AI selloff. $LITE CEO seconds $IREN CEO: massive disconnect between demand books and market action amid massive selloff (Fri, Nov 6). $AMD CEO (CNBC): at the very beginning of the adoption cycle; seeing demand higher than supply (Wed, Nov 4). $ARM CEO on whether AI infra spending is peaking: if the US hesitates on data center buildout, other places will pick it up; huge global demand; US wants to compete vs China which is not slowing (Thu, Nov 5). $APLD CEO: bank construction lending, project financing and bond markets remain available though rates and spreads have risen; big risk is delivering on time; demand picture extremely robust (Mon, Nov 2). $QCOM CEO: not demand bound but supply bound; short on wafers, substrate, assembly because demand is very high; market reacting to the speed of the compute buildout. $META on political risk: a governor who campaigned on data centers won and wants to meet so as not to lose the investment/jobs; even politicians campaigning against DCs are negotiating, but Meta's president emphasized starting new US DCs will become more difficult (Wed, Nov 4). TLDR: Extremely bullish commentary from accelerator players, networking, and semi companies; DC developers report strong pricing/demand but increasingly emphasize financing, permitting and execution risks (more cautious); politicians previously against DCs showing cooperation. Important difference between demand disappearing and demand delayed by inability to build fast enough. Despite the severity of the selloff, not seeing evidence of widespread AI demand destruction in commentary.

Critique of repeated "AI Bubble" label; infra names (memory $ESMT, optical $POET/$COHR/$LITE) not fundamentally bubbly

Status ID: 2119363124147191823 · 2026-11-08 09:35 UTC · Original post by @aleabitoreddit

Traditional Chinese: Serenity 認為「AI 泡沫」一詞被重複使用很愚蠢。在基礎設施名稱中：從 Samsung 到 $ESMT 的記憶體公司前向本益比 1-3倍，HBM 玩家現有超大規模多年價格下限 LTA；光學公司如 $POET 交易價低於淨現金 9700 萬美元，將營運業務估值為負值甚至低於零；而比例較高的 AI 公司持續記錄未來 3-5 年需求，如與 $COHR 簽署的新 200 億美元 LTA 到與 $LITE 的 50 億美元 LTA。媒體如何能看到負企業價值高波動光學公司（兩年燃燒率仍使公司估值低於零）或單位數本益比記憶體公司（有合約需求與價格下限）卻說是泡沫？許多這些名稱今天極度盈利。其他元素如某些私人估值看起來像泡沫的初創（Instinct）、未正確融資的槓桿式 Neoclouds、AI 實驗室估值與承諾應謹慎。但一切被歸為一類。估值可因宏觀、監管不確定性或資本支出減少而壓縮或修正，但對實際支撑 AI 建設的公司，更像 2026 年的「大 AI 去槓桿化」而非基本需求毀滅。隨著財報與自由現金流積累，對瓶頸恢復有信心，市場最終會區分耐久性與成長與泡沫估值。

English: Serenity argues it's stupid that "AI Bubble" gets used over and over. In infra names: 1-3x fwd P/E memory companies from Samsung to $ESMT, with HBM players having hyperscaler multi-year price floor LTAs; optical companies like $POET traded -$97M under net cash, valuing the operating business negatively; while AI companies with larger premiums continue to record demand over the next 3-5 years, such as new $20B LTAs with $COHR to $5B LTAs with $LITE. Questions how media can see negative EV high-beta opticals (where 2 years of burn would still value below $0) or single-digit memory companies with contracted demand + price floors and call it a bubble. Many names are extremely profitable today. Other elements exist (certain startups like Instinct looking like private valuation bubbles, leveraged Neoclouds that didn't finance properly, AI lab valuations/commitments to view cautiously), but everything gets grouped together. Valuations can compress from macro, regulatory uncertainty, or capex reductions, but for companies physically powering the AI buildout, it looks more like the Great AI Deleveraging of 2026 than fundamental demand destruction. Confident in recovery especially in bottlenecks as earnings and FCF accumulate, and markets eventually discern durability + growth over frothy valuations.

Contrarian take on Neoclouds like $NBIS after valuation crash; theme looks compelling

Status ID: 2119077473107472729 · 2026-11-07 14:40 UTC · Original post by @aleabitoreddit

Traditional Chinese: Serenity 提出對 Neoclouds（如 $NBIS）的逆向觀點，認為估值崩盤後主題看起來很有吸引力。Elon 的 $SPCX 重點關注這個市場，因為向 $GOOGL 和 Anthropic 出售算力後看到經濟學很吸引，SpaceX 最近為 GPU 採購融資 40B。$AMZN、$MSFT、$META 也在加速 AI DC 建設。如果 Neocloud 有與 $NVDA 等的緊密關係、現有簽約產能與許可、資金充足的對手方（如超大規模）、正確的融資經濟學（不像 $CRWV 高息債務），那麼會表現不錯。更大的擔憂是新玩家開始融資或許可過程，進入障礙現在極高。$IREN 財報顯示公共 Neoclouds 能適應緊市場（90% 預付款、舊 H100 重新簽約價格翻倍）。更嚴格的融資和許可會增加已建立 Neoclouds 的競爭優勢，形成更大護城河。

English: Serenity gives a contrarian take on Neoclouds like $NBIS, saying the theme looks compelling after the valuation crash. Elon's $SPCX is focused on this market because the economics are attractive after selling compute to $GOOGL and Anthropic, reflected in SpaceX's $40B financing for GPUs. Hyperscalers $AMZN, $MSFT, $META are accelerating AI DC buildouts. If a Neocloud has close relationships with $NVDA for allocations, existing contracted capacity + permitting, well-funded counterparties, and attractive financing (not high-interest debt like $CRWV), they will do fine. Bigger worry for newer players as barriers to entry are now extremely high. $IREN earnings show public Neoclouds can adapt (90% prepayments for GPU capex, older H100s recontracted at double pricing). Tighter financing and restrictive permitting would increase the competitive advantage of established neoclouds and create a larger moat.
