原文摘要 / Original Summary: Now that we're close to 2027 it's a good idea to stress test downside scenarios where you have: 1. Capex cuts past 2028 2. Stranded Compute / DC Slowdowns 3. Finance deterioration (base case) 4. 2x+ rate hikes. Working in parallel and see what happens with valuations of AI longs. If you see acceleration despite all 4, I'd argue that's an opportunity. Next-gen of photonics is one of the rare segments with an extreme rise in optical content and ASPs that can offset aggregate capex cuts. Everyone’s moving into that space, including Elon Musk doing M&A, Marvell, Samsung and others with M&A… and we haven’t seen economic impact on emerging architectures even hit players from Celestial to Ayar + their suppliers yet. If we look at a scenario where there's excess GPUs but power constraints: We might see slowdown in aggregate accelerator/GPU/ASIC procurement, but your optical engines + light sources + switches players see acceleration. Especially as incentive to replace power inefficient networking... since different (cheap BOM) optical architectures improves GPU utilization or even HBM requirements. So value migrates to players more high-beta to architectural shifts. If Celestial projections are $500m -> $1B, and we expect -30% aggregate capex cuts, I'd argue we'd still see upward revisions from Celestial over the next 2 years. But financing with #3 matters a lot as seen with $AAOI dropping off of a $550M, vs. $COHR getting prepayments for capacity expansion from their customers. Companies that rely on external foundries might be less sensitive to #3 since they're not as capex intensive. On the other hand, companies in sectors have a lot of floating interest like certain Neoclouds, and require further financing might be the most impacted. Legacy and specialty memory is a different cycle that markets conflate with AI capex. If consumer demand weakens but at the same time, players like Windbond exits DDR2, pricing durability is longer than markets expect. Wafer/substrate procurement such as ABF, SOI, InP are low cost but large downstream destruction if not secured, so we’ll likely see acceleration here as seen with $NVDA earnings call. Qualification cycle players within bottlenecks remain very compelling, since first revenue in a large TAM, relative to MC is material to that company. If a company is valued at $400m then they get a $200m of orders, it's more asymmetrical to a certain company than a rounding error to a $50B incumbent revenue. There’s period of times where prices are dislocated from fundamentals from leveraged players getting wiped. I’d argue that time is now. I disagree with a lot of the stupid "it's over takes" but I agree it's a good idea to haircut future capex expectations and forward valuations due to financing tightening + macro. It's also good idea to see the nuances and stress test downside cases. But TLDR: I'd argue next phase of AI infrastructure is going to be increasingly disconnected from aggregate AI capex growth. I just personally wrote 4 things I'm using to stress test jointly some of the names I'm looking at, so I'm more comfortable waiting things out... despite seeing hedge funds getting liquidated left and right and overwhelming short positioning. I personally see acceleration in more narrow parts of the supply chain, while markets treat the entire AI trade as one. We'll see how things play out on longer horizons.

AAOI 空頭 / Short: 管理層失去可信度，再次啟動 $550M ATM 稀釋，與先前關於其他融資方法及股價謹慎的評論相反。操作上 CPO 與光收發器仍具吸引力，但串行稀釋難以支持。Management lost credibility with another $550M ATM contrary to earlier financing commentary; operationally compelling on CPO/transceivers but serial dilution hard to support.

COHR 多頭 / Long: 從客戶預付款獲得產能擴張資金，相對於 AAOI 的融資方式較佳。Gets prepayments for capacity expansion from customers, better financing relative to AAOI ATM.

Photonics / optical engines, light sources, switches (e.g. Celestial, Ayar) 多頭 / Long (in downside scenarios): 對架構轉變高貝塔，即使整體資本支出削減下仍可能加速；光學內容與 ASP 上升可抵銷。High-beta to architectural shifts; can accelerate despite aggregate capex cuts due to optical content/ASP rise.

Legacy/specialty memory (e.g. Windbond) 中立 / Neutral to Long: 不同週期，與 AI 資本支出混淆；DDR2 退出等使定價耐久性較長。Different cycle; pricing durability longer than expected.

Wafer/substrate (ABF, SOI, InP) 多頭 / Long: 低成本但未確保有下遊大風險，可能見加速（如 NVDA 所示）。Likely acceleration as low cost but critical downstream.

Neoclouds 空頭 / Short (financing sensitive): 大量浮動利息且需進一步融資，受 #3 影響最大。Most impacted by finance deterioration.

Qualification cycle players in bottlenecks 多頭 / Long: 首次收入相對市值不對稱，具吸引力。Compelling asymmetry.
