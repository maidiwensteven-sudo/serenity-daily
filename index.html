Rampant deleveraging: $CIFR down 44% post colo deals, $AAOI back to pre-orders price despite capacity raise & forecasts; $ESMT as memory sector example with 1.6x annualized P/E, asset-heavy, DDR2/3 supply tightening

Status ID: 2118015527440515458 · 2026-11-04 16:20 UTC · Original post by @aleabitoreddit
Little rant, but to show you how rampant deleveraging has become: - $CIFR is worth 44% LESS than when they announced their initial $GOOGL / $AMZN colo deals. - $AAOI is now ~ the same price BEFORE they secured major hyperscaler orders, raised $1.6B for capacity expansion and gave their suprising initial -> $378M+ projection -> ~$471M revenue forecast. - $ESMT ($2B MC) is the perfect example representing the memory sector. B-BUT AI NAMES ARE NOT PROFITABLE??? Q3 Earnings: $526.2M gross profit, $330M net income. To give you a comparison of what that looks like, the net income of $CRWD ($251B MC) was literally $5.3M. $NOW ($113B MC) was $277M. $TSLA ($1.5T) was $787M. Different industries/durability, but if we care about what's actually hitting the balance sheet: ESMT is sitting on ~$937M in net assets ($740M net cash, $278M inventory, etc), so EV goes down to $1.2B ish. Annualized P/E: 1.6x... People keep saying "Peak Earnings", "Cyclical", "no moat" but who in the world would want to build an old ass DDR2 line + fab and qualify it? From ESMT commentary, we'll likely see ASP hikes Q4 to make up for potential consumer demand dropping. And further supply tightening with Samsung/Micron discontinuing DDR2/DDR3 lines. Winbond was leaving DDR2 lines to pursue higher end memory. People keep saying China, but even Gigadevice was leaving DDR3 to pursue higher end memory. "DDR3's share of our DRAM revenue will continue to decline until we eventually stop selling DDR3 products."Nanya discontinued DDR2 lines few days ago. This is while majority of ESMT revenue comes from DDR2/DDR3. In ~3 quarters of constant earnings, ESMT would have more assets than their entire MC. Who is going to stop buying $40 DLink cameras if their $3 memory cost goes to $3.5? $MU / Samsung / $SKHY / $SNDK share similar parallels where durability is contractually enforced via take/pay LTAs with price floors. But markets are pretending that it just won't happen, despite Samsung already being the most profitable company in the world. Markets should care about how much FCF is actually hitting the balance sheets, but are just indiscriminately selling off everything now. But continue rotating capital into 47x P/E companies like Walmart or 180x forward P/E names like $CRWD.

$AAOI signs $194.4M/year contract with Yuunan Germanium for InP substrates; 5-year implied ~$970M; capacity expansion signals, positive $AXTI read-through

Status ID: 2117664715811995717 · 2026-11-03 17:06 UTC · Original post by @aleabitoreddit
$AAOI signs $194.4M/year contract with Yuunan Germanium. This is for 2027 alone, with a 5 year contract. Given an implied total of ~$970M in InP substrates over 5 years (before price hikes) Hmm just some thoughts: - AOI laser capacity expansion seems bigger than what’s disclosed based on procurement patterns? 180K wafers seems like an extreme amount compared to what’s needed for lasers within their own transceivers… if they can consume all of that So maybe we’ll hear about a new UHP laser contracts in earnings, after $LITE announced a $5B contract. - read through on $AXTI is positive, given they’ll probably be #1 in InP substrates manufacturing scale in the future + have contracts with $LITE and $COHR. - positive AOI was able to secure capacity during the bottleneck More spend is probably the boogeyman in the AI trade, but it’s a good signal for earnings.

繁體中文： Serenity 指出 $AAOI 與 Yuunan Germanium 簽署每年 1.944 億美元的合約。這僅針對 2027 年，為五年合約。隱含五年內 InP 基板總額約 9.7 億美元（價格上漲前）。她思考：AOI 雷射產能擴張似乎大於已披露的，基於採購模式；18 萬片晶圓相較於其自身收發器所需雷射來說是極大數量，如果能全部消耗。因此可能在財報中聽到新的 UHP 雷射合約，在 $LITE 宣佈 50 億美元合約後。對 $AXTI 的讀取是正面的，因為他們可能在未來成為 InP 基板製造規模第一，並與 $LITE 和 $COHR 有合約。正面的是 AOI 能在瓶頸期間確保產能。更多支出可能是 AI 交易的恐懼，但對財報是好信號。
