TSMC Nikkei Forum: insatiable leading-node demand, no AI slowdown, demand far above supply

Status ID: 2120488757334044945 · 2026-11-11 12:08 UTC · Original post by @aleabitoreddit

Traditional Chinese: Serenity 指出今天從 $CRWV 聽到 10x 計算需求 > 供應。今日從 $TSM 在 Nikkei Forum 聽到：「我們領先節點（最先進）硅的需求是無法滿足的」。客戶「想要掌握任何他們能得到的計算能力。這真的是一個無法滿足的環境。」問：AI 需求是否放緩？答：「我們沒有看到任何放緩跡象」。問：需求與 TSMC 供應能力比較？答：「需求實際上仍遠高於我們今天的供應能力。」TSMC 指出電力與基板為客戶遇到的瓶頸。關於 2024 至 2029 年 AI 加速器收入中高 50 年代年化成長估計，Wei 說「可能會更高」。從傳統保守公司描述為「無法滿足」……看起來不太像任何 AI 需求崩潰。

English: Serenity notes you heard 10x demand for compute > supply from $CRWV today. Hearing from $TSM today on Nikkei Forum: "The demand on our leading node [most advanced] silicon is insatiable". Customers "want to get [their] hands around any compute capability they can have. It's truly an insatiable environment." Q: Whether AI demand was slowing? A: "we did not see any sign of slowdown". Q: How demand compared with TSMC’s ability to supply: "the demand actually is still far above our capability today to supply." TSMC identified power and substrates as bottlenecks customers were encountering. On the mid-to-high-50s annualized growth estimate for AI accelerator revenue from 2024 to 2029, Wei said "it probably will be higher." Describing things as "insatiable" from a traditionally conservative company... doesn't quite look like any collapse in AI demand.
