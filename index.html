English: I've been talking with industry executives about sentiment around Jensen after the controversy at $NVDA. The way it was told to me, Jensen's word was enough for the supplier to deploy its resources, speed up the expansion, and set aside capacity for Nvidia. For every little component of the supply chain, it almost sounded like Jensen would visit local factory to factory himself... just to accelerate Nvidia's roadmap. We also had discussions about other CEOs like $ARM and replacements. But also factoring in Jensen's local roots, deep technical knowledge... It felt it would be difficult to replace the years of trust and personal relationships that he had to rally supply chains around Nvidia's ambitious architectural roadmaps.
