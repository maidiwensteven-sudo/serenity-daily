$SIVE supply chain thesis, laser capacity, and management messaging

Status ID: 2113950041207607453 · 2026-10-24 11:05 UTC · Original post by @aleabitoreddit
Yes, I like $SIVE. It's a fun company to cover, since there's a lot of supply chain alpha here. But the company completely Tall Poppy Syndromed itself last earnings. And it's clearly shown in the drawdown. ... Sivers has 100M CW DFB laser capacity coming online Q4 2027 during an industry shortage. ... There's nothing I see remotely close to $SIVE, in terms of % of global CW laser capacity they're coming online with relative to MC. They have a ton commercialization paths, from O-Net, $POET, $JBL, Ayar, $GFS, $AAPL, $AEVA, and many potential hyperscaler/pluggable engagements. Even if one or two fall through, the breadth of ongoing quals is so deep + current laser bottleneck is severe, that it's more likely than not many will convert to production.

繁體中文： Serenity 回應關於 $SIVE 的信心問題，表示喜歡覆蓋這家公司，因為供應鏈上有許多 alpha。但指出公司在上季財報完全自己「Tall Poppy Syndrome」（過度謙虚或錯誤宣傳），導致回撤。主要論點：Sivers 在 Q4 2027 將上線 100M CW DFB 雷射產能，正值行業短缺；已有大量晶圓代工配額，可立刻變現瓶頸。瓶頸使客戶資格轉換更快、機率更高。提到模組製造商扩大供應商數量（LightCounting 指出從 2-3 家增至 5-7+），Innolight 等正在尋找新供應商並計劃簽 2-3 年長約鎖定上遊產能。同時 $COHR、$LITE（賣到 2029）、Landmark、$AAOI 等已將雷射轉向自己方案或銷售完畢。Sivers 正與中國可插拔玩家及超級雲端有持續接觸（包括 $JBL 宣佈前）。對收入模型指出不能對近零 NRE 收入直接套 25-35% CAGR；產能可能擴至 ~300M。提及 $LITE 單一 2028 廠房引用 $5B 雷射產能。Sivers 目標長期混合毛利 ~50-60%+ 。解釋為何目前無收入：2024 前專注 CPO 等客製開發（$AEVA 近期轉換、$AAPL 持續、Ayar 等 2028 量產）；2024 CEO 轉向後加上大量商業化產品（$JBL、$GFS 等）。投點收斂於 2027 以後。批評管理層迅息與近期執行：過度強調小型合約（Seminex、AllSpace、Tachyon），西方受眾不關心；未承諾 NASDAQ 上市時間表。建議下月財報糾正溝通，強調雷射產能轉換的百億經濟規模與執行。總結：相對市值而言，即將上線的全球 CW 雷射產能占比無出其右；商業化路徑廣（O-Net、$POET、$JBL、Ayar、$GFS、$AAPL、$AEVA 等），即使一兩個失敗，瓶頸厳重下多數可能轉為產量。

English: Serenity responds to a question on confidence in $SIVE, stating she likes the company because of substantial supply-chain alpha, but notes it Tall Poppy Syndromed itself last earnings, contributing to the drawdown. Key points: Sivers has 100M CW DFB laser capacity coming online Q4 2027 during an industry shortage, with large foundry allocations (Win Semi + others from 2023/2024 quals) enabling monetization of the bottleneck today. Bottlenecks accelerate qualification conversion. Module makers are expanding suppliers (LightCounting: from 2-3 to 5-7+); Innolight and peers are introducing new suppliers and planning 2-3 year contracts to lock capacity. Meanwhile $COHR, $LITE (sold out into 2029), Landmark, and $AAOI have redirected or sold out lasers. Sivers has ongoing engagements with Chinese pluggable players and hyperscalers (including pre-$JBL). Revenue modeling critique: cannot apply 25-35% CAGR to near-zero NRE; capacity may scale toward ~300M. References $LITE citing $5B laser capacity from one 2028 fab. Sivers targets ~50-60%+ long-term blended gross margins. Explains lack of current revenue: pre-2024 focus on custom CPO/lidar (recent $AEVA conversion, ongoing $AAPL, Ayar and others targeting 2028 volume); post-2024 CEO shift added mass-commercialization products for pluggables ($JBL, $GFS, etc.). Inflection points converge from 2027. Critiques management messaging and near-term execution: over-emphasis on small contracts (Seminex, AllSpace, Tachyon) that Western audiences ignore; lack of committed NASDAQ listing timeline. Suggests next earnings as opportunity to correct communication and emphasize the economic scale of capacity conversion. Conclusion: nothing comparable in % of global CW laser capacity relative to market cap; broad commercialization paths (O-Net, $POET, $JBL, Ayar, $GFS, $AAPL, $AEVA, and potential hyperscaler/pluggable deals) make multiple conversions likely amid the severe bottleneck.
