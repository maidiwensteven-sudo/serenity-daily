- 10月22日是自COVID崩盤以來首次，高盛追蹤的多空、系統性與多策略對沖基金在單日全部虧損超過1%。

|| Pretty crazy Reuters piece on the global selloff.
- October 22 marked the 1st time since the COVID crash that long/short, systematic, and multi-strategy hedge funds tracked by Goldman Sachs ALL lost more than 1% in a single day.
- Tech-focused hedge funds: down ~10% in October
- Asia-focused hedge funds: down ~9% in October
There’s separate interviews where Citadel/JP Morgan have been saying retail has been the ones buying the dip in semiconductor, neocloud, and memory stocks.

Quite the divergence, I do think there’s been a ton of irrational selling now, hedge fund deleveraging seems to be a major contributor.
