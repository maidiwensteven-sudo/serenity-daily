Phison CEO commentary + context behind $SNDK / Kioxia selloff regarding Trendforce revisions: Pua attributed China's recent enterprise SSD demand slowdown to GPU shortages since November 2026. Without GPUs, additional purchases offer little value, prompting some companies to sell server components on the spot market to recover cash. TLDR: delayed procurement from GPU shortages; actual order cuts but deferred not structural demand weakening. Phison sees inventories insufficient for 2027 demand as opportunity for NAND accumulation. Bottleneck in China migrated back to GPUs.
