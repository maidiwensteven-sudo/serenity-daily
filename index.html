AMZN CEO comments validating Neoclouds and memory shortages beyond 2027

Status ID: 2114051018510287340 · 2026-10-24 17:46 UTC · Original post by @aleabitoreddit
So back on your Neoclouds like $NBIS + memory read through... From $AMZN CEO + A16z interview. Q: On memory shortages (eg. $MU / SK Hynix / Samsung): A: "There's only so much memory, and I don't think that that shortage ends in 2027." Q: On AWS capacity: A: "A good chunk of 28 is sold out", "largely sold out through 2027." Q: On capex ROI: A: Amazon had said it would spend about $220 billion in capex for 2026. But Jassy said the company "could invest a lot more and it wouldn't be a bad use of capital" This is probably validating economics of Neoclouds model since this comes from a pretty stingy hyperscaler like Amazon... TLDR: Not really too much groundbreaking, just incremental validation of what was said previous earnings. Broader shortages (like memory) won't end in 2027. Always positive to hear from $AMZN on insatiable demand + ROI given atrocious Neocloud sentiment right now.

繁體中文： Serenity 引用 Amazon CEO Andy Jassy 與 A16z 訪談內容，作為 Neocloud（如 $NBIS）與記憶體的讀穿。關於記憶體短缺（例如 $MU / SK Hynix / Samsung）：「只有這麼多記憶體，我不認為短缺會在 2027 年結束。」關於 AWS 產能：「很大一部分 2028 已售出」、「大致售完至 2027。」關於資本支出：Amazon 曾表示 2026 年約支出 $220B，但 Jassy 說公司「可以投資更多，且不會是不良資本運用」。這被視為來自節億 hyperscaler 的 Neocloud 模式經濟性驗證。AWS 宣稱較可比 AI 加速器有約 30-40% 更好的價格效能；Annapurna 被評為公司歷史上最佳收購之一。TLDR：並非重大突破，僅為先前財報的增量驗證；更廣泛短缺（如記憶體）不會在 2027 結束；在 Neocloud 情緒極差時聽到 $AMZN 對無盡需求與 ROI 的正面評論是正面的。

English: Serenity highlights comments from Amazon CEO Andy Jassy in an A16z interview as a read-through for Neoclouds such as $NBIS and memory. On memory shortages (e.g., $MU / SK Hynix / Samsung): “There's only so much memory, and I don't think that that shortage ends in 2027.” On AWS capacity: “A good chunk of 28 is sold out,” “largely sold out through 2027.” On capex ROI: Amazon had said ~$220 billion for 2026, but Jassy said the company “could invest a lot more and it wouldn't be a bad use of capital.” Views this as validating the economics of the Neocloud model from a stingy hyperscaler. Notes AWS claims approximately 30-40% better price-performance than comparable AI accelerators and praise for the Annapurna acquisition. TLDR: not groundbreaking, just incremental validation of prior earnings commentary; broader shortages like memory won't end in 2027; always positive to hear from $AMZN on insatiable demand + ROI given atrocious Neocloud sentiment right now.
