Jensen Huang on photonics scale-up (15x) and laser demand implications

Status ID: 2114213351060554010 · 2026-10-25 04:31 UTC · Original post by @aleabitoreddit
$NVDA Jensen Huang on photonics: adding scale-up to optical domain, “is going to be, well, 15x” to aggregate volume. Relating to $LITE, $COHR, and laser companies.. “I'm fairly certain the amount of optics that we need in the future will be several orders of magnitude” Huang added. We’re already in a CW laser bottleneck, but given the time/rate for capacity expansion, I definitely don’t see it meeting the scale Jensen envisions… Your leading merchant suppliers focusing new capacity toward UHP CPO lasers might also unintentionally make shortages around lower end ranges more severe… Since even if you don’t reallocate capacity (which Coherent implied they’d do to maximize margins), the demand for lower end ranges increase too as pluggable demand grows. Anyway, you’re gonna need a lot of lasers and given low BOM cost… this might trigger some fun game theory allocation wars + price hikes for supply

繁體中文： Serenity 引述 $NVDA Jensen Huang 關於光子學的評論：將 scale-up 加入光學領域「將是，嗯，15 倍」的總量。並提到未來所需光學量將是數個數量級。她指出目前已處於 CW 雷射瓶頸，但產能擴張時間與速率可能無法滿足 Jensen 所想像的規模。領先的 merchant 供應商將新產能聚焦於 UHP CPO 雷射，可能無意中加劇低端範圍短缺；即使不重新配置產能（Coherent 曾暗示為最大化利潤而做），隨著可插拔需求成長，低端需求也會增加。低 BOM 成本下可能引發分配博弈與價格上漲。涉及 $LITE、$COHR 與雷射公司。

English: Serenity highlights $NVDA Jensen Huang comments on photonics: adding scale-up to the optical domain “is going to be, well, 15x” to aggregate volume, and “I'm fairly certain the amount of optics that we need in the future will be several orders of magnitude.” She notes the industry is already in a CW laser bottleneck, and given the time/rate for capacity expansion she does not see it meeting the scale Jensen envisions. Leading merchant suppliers focusing new capacity toward UHP CPO lasers might unintentionally make shortages around lower end ranges more severe (Coherent implied reallocation to maximize margins); even without reallocation, lower-end demand rises as pluggable demand grows. Low BOM cost could trigger allocation wars and price hikes. Relates to $LITE, $COHR, and laser companies.

AMZN CEO comments validating Neoclouds and memory shortages beyond 2027

Status ID: 2114051018510287340 · 2026-10-24 17:46 UTC · Original post by @aleabitoreddit
So back on your Neoclouds like $NBIS + memory read through... From $AMZN CEO + A16z interview. Q: On memory shortages (eg. $MU / SK Hynix / Samsung): A: "There's only so much memory, and I don't think that that shortage ends in 2027." Q: On AWS capacity: A: "A good chunk of 28 is sold out", "largely sold out through 2027." Q: On capex ROI: A: Amazon had said it would spend about $220 billion in capex for 2026. But Jassy said the company "could invest a lot more and it wouldn't be a bad use of capital" This is probably validating economics of Neoclouds model since this comes from a pretty stingy hyperscaler like Amazon... TLDR: Not really too much groundbreaking, just incremental validation of what was said previous earnings. Broader shortages (like memory) won't end in 2027. Always positive to hear from $AMZN on insatiable demand + ROI given atrocious Neocloud sentiment right now.

繁體中文： Serenity 引用 Amazon CEO Andy Jassy 與 A16z 訪談內容，作為 Neocloud（如 $NBIS）與記憶體的讀穿。關於記憶體短缺（例如 $MU / SK Hynix / Samsung）：「只有這麼多記憶體，我不認為短缺會在 2027 年結束。」關於 AWS 產能：「很大一部分 2028 已售出」、「大致售完至 2027。」關於資本支出：Amazon 曾表示 2026 年約支出 $220B，但 Jassy 說公司「可以投資更多，且不會是不良資本運用」。這被視為來自節儉 hyperscaler 的 Neocloud 模式經濟性驗證。AWS 宣稱較可比 AI 加速器有約 30-40% 更好的價格效能；Annapurna 被評為公司歷史上最佳收購之一。TLDR：並非重大突破，僅為先前財報的增量驗證；更廣泛短缺（如記憶體）不會在 2027 結束；在 Neocloud 情緒極差時聽到 $AMZN 對無盡需求與 ROI 的正面評論是正面的。

English: Serenity highlights comments from Amazon CEO Andy Jassy in an A16z interview as a read-through for Neoclouds such as $NBIS and memory. On memory shortages (e.g., $MU / SK Hynix / Samsung): “There's only so much memory, and I don't think that that shortage ends in 2027.” On AWS capacity: “A good chunk of 28 is sold out,” “largely sold out through 2027.” On capex ROI: Amazon had said ~$220 billion for 2026, but Jassy said the company “could invest a lot more and it wouldn't be a bad use of capital.” Views this as validating the economics of the Neocloud model from a stingy hyperscaler. Notes AWS claims approximately 30-40% better price-performance than comparable AI accelerators and praise for the Annapurna acquisition. TLDR: not groundbreaking, just incremental validation of prior earnings commentary; broader shortages like memory won't end in 2027; always positive to hear from $AMZN on insatiable demand + ROI given atrocious Neocloud sentiment right now.
