Phison CEO commentary today + more context behind $SNDK / Kioxia selloff regarding Trendforce revisions: Pua attributed China's recent enterprise SSD demand slowdown to GPU shortages since November 2026. The principal challenge in 2027 would be insufficient supply rather than weakening demand... TLDR: delayed procurement from GPU shortages. It's interesting the bottleneck in China migrated back to GPUs though.
